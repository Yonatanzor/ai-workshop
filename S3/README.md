# Session 3 — One Paper, Many Audiences

**Type:** 90-minute session  
**Audience:** First-semester MD-PhD students  
**Data tier:** Tier 1 (published paper, public tools — NotebookLM, Gamma)

---

## What students leave with

The ability to use AI to summarise and present a primary paper for different audiences, and the habit of checking that every claim the AI makes actually appears in the source.

## The source paper

> Musunuru K, et al. Patient-specific in vivo gene editing to treat a rare genetic disease. *New England Journal of Medicine*, 2025.

One infant (KJ) with severe CPS1 deficiency — a urea-cycle disorder. Six months from diagnosis to a bespoke base-editing therapy designed for his own mutation. Three infusions, February–April 2025.

**Rule for the session:** every claim about this treatment must come from this paper. Background sources are for context only.

## Background sources (context only)

| Paper | Role |
|---|---|
| Komor et al., *Nature* 2016 | First base editor |
| Musunuru et al., *Nature* 2021 | Base editing in monkey liver |
| Gropman & Komor, *NEJM* 2025 | Editorial on the KJ paper |

## Learning outcomes

- Use NotebookLM to synthesise and query a primary paper
- Use Gamma to build a short presentation aimed at a specific audience
- Audit an AI-generated summary: does each claim appear in the paper it cites, and what did the report leave out?

## Session structure

| Phase | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 10 min | The paper, the tools, the task |
| **Make** | 20 min | Each group turns the paper into one product in NotebookLM (lay summary, clinical brief, grant background section, or science communication piece) |
| **Defend** | 20 min | An AI plays the target audience and asks its hardest questions — groups respond |
| **Present** | 40 min | Each group shows both parts in a short Gamma presentation |

## Before the session

Students should sign in to **NotebookLM** (any Google account) and **Gamma** (gamma.app) before arriving. Both have free tiers that cover all session exercises.

## Critical-thinking challenge

**Citation audit.** After the presentations, each group takes one claim from another group's NotebookLM summary and traces it back to the paper. They must answer: does the source actually say that, or did NotebookLM add something the paper does not contain? What did the report leave out entirely?
