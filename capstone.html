Capstone Showcase

6-minute live demos. What you built, what broke, and what you learned.

90 min 🎓 Final session ★ Showcase day
What to bring
Your working (or broken) AI workflow
One entry from your failure log
A named data tier
Your verification plan
No slides required
Overview Format Challenge Gallery

Learning objectives

  1. Present a working (or instructively broken) AI workflow to peers and instructors
  2. Reflect on what you learned across the full 9-session arc
  3. Give and receive rigorous critique — one sharp question per demo
  4. Document what you would do differently if you started again
Note The capstone is not about polish. A broken tool with an honest postmortem is worth more than a smooth demo with no reflection. Show your work — including the parts that failed.

The 6-minute format

0 – 2 min
The problem
What research problem does this solve? Why AI, and not a spreadsheet?
2 – 4 min
Live demo
Show it running. Name the data tier. Show at least one real output.
4 – 5 min
What broke
One honest failure from your log. What did you learn from it?
5 – 6 min
Q&A
One sharp question from a peer, assigned in advance.

Evaluation rubric

Criterion Strong Acceptable Needs work
Does it run? Live demo, real output Screenshots only Not shown
Data tier Named and justified Named, not justified Not addressed
Verification Tested, evidence shown Plan described Not addressed
Failure log Specific, insightful Mentioned Not addressed

Critical-thinking challenge

Each presenter is assigned a peer reviewer in advance. The reviewer’s job: ask the one question the presenter least wants to hear. No softballs. The question is recorded in both participants’ failure logs.

Before the session Review your partner’s S8 project scope (task, data tier, verification plan) and prepare one sharp critical question. Write it down before you arrive.

4. Commit and push. The gallery updates immediately.
