The Five Questions

Ask these of every tool, in every session, until they are a habit.

01
What was it trained on, or given to read?
Identify the data the model has access to — and what it doesn't.
02
How would I know if this is wrong?
Every output needs a verification strategy before it is used.
03
Who or what is missing from its data?
Gaps in training data become gaps in outputs — sometimes invisible ones.
04
What did using it cost — privacy, time, or a skill I did not practise?
Every tool use has a cost beyond the subscription fee.
05
Would a non-AI method do this better?
AI is not always the right tool. The four-test card sort applies here too.

Data Confidentiality Tiers

Course rule
No identifiable patient data and no unpublished lab data in any public AI tool. Check, for each tool, whether you are signed in with a personal or an institutional account.
Tier 1
Published papers & public datasets
AI: Any approved tool.
Human: Spot-check output and sources.
Tier 2
De-identified research data
AI: Structure only; code runs where data lives.
Human: Verify results a second way.
Tier 3
Identifiable patient or unpublished lab data
AI: Nothing in public tools.
Human: Approve every input and output.
Tier 4
Anything affecting a patient decision
AI: Drafts only, never actions.
Human: A qualified clinician decides.
