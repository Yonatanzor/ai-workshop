Learning Objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

  1. Name the main families of AI use in research and medicine
  2. Distinguish open from closed models and explain why it matters for confidential data
  3. Apply four tests to decide whether a task calls for AI, traditional software, or both

Before the Session

  • How a language model produces text (prediction, not lookup)
  • Machine learning vs. deep learning

The Activity

A map of the AI landscape for working scientists and clinicians. Students sort real research and clinical tasks into three piles — AI, traditional software, or hybrid — using four concrete tests. The session establishes the vocabulary and mental model that every later session builds on, and introduces the data-confidentiality tier system that governs every activity in the course.
Hugging Face

Critical-Thinking Challenge

Headline audit: four AI claims from abstracts or news. What evidence would you need to believe each one? Red flags: accuracy >99%, no external test set, no simple baseline comparison.
