Learning Objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

  1. Use NotebookLM to synthesise and query a primary paper for a specific audience
  2. Build a short Gamma presentation from AI-generated material
  3. Audit an AI summary: does each claim appear in the paper it cites?

Before the Session

  • Musunuru et al. Patient-specific in vivo gene editing to treat a rare genetic disease. NEJM 2025. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2504747
⚙️ Before the session
Sign in to NotebookLM (any Google account) and Gamma (gamma.app) before the session. Both have free tiers.

The Activity

One landmark paper, seven different products for seven different audiences — in 90 minutes. Students use NotebookLM to synthesise the paper for their assigned audience, then an AI plays that audience and asks its hardest questions. The session ends with a Gamma presentation and a citation audit: does each AI-generated claim actually appear in the paper it cites?
NotebookLMGamma

Critical-Thinking Challenge

Citation audit: take one claim from another group's NotebookLM summary and trace it back to the paper. Does the source actually say that? What did the report leave out?
