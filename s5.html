Learning Objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

  1. Implement a TF-IDF retrieval step over a small corpus of PDFs
  2. Understand how retrieved chunks affect the model's answer
  3. Identify three failure modes: wrong chunk, answer not in corpus, model confabulates

Before the Session

  • What is retrieval-augmented generation?
  • Le et al. Mismatch-repair deficiency predicts response of solid tumors to PD-1 blockade. Science 2017. DOI: 10.1126/science.aan6733

The Activity

Google ColabNVIDIA APIpypdfscikit-learn
ℹ️ Instructor note
The seven papers must be in a folder called 'papers' next to the notebook. Access them through your institutional library — do not upload copyrighted PDFs to public AI tools.

Critical-Thinking Challenge

Pick one answer the pipeline gave and manually check the retrieved chunk it used. Does the chunk actually support the answer, or did the model add something that isn't there?
