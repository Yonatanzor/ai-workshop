Learning Objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

  1. Scope an AI workflow that solves a real problem in your own research
  2. Check the data tier before anything is sent to a tool
  3. Plan how you will verify that the workflow works

Before the Session

  • No specific pre-reading required.

The Activity

The last working session before the showcase. Students look back across seven sessions, choose one AI workflow to adopt as a real project, and take their first step — in the room, with instructors present. The session ends with each group naming their task, their data tier, their verification plan, and their biggest worry.
